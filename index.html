Room 622 - Building 78

Brisbane, Australia

I’m a second-year Computer Science PhD candidate at The University of Queensland, supervised by Professor Gianluca Demartini, Dr. Joel Mackenzie, and Dr. Kevin Roitero.

My research broadly focuses on understanding bias and information representation in large language models (LLMs), with particular interests in political and perspective bias, relevance assessment, multilinguality, and model internals. Outside of my specific research interests, I’m mostly just very curious and enjoy learning about things I know little about. If you have an interesting idea, project, or problem you’d like to chat about, I’m always happy to hear about it — and if you happen to run into me at a conference, definitely come say hi!

My recent works and collaborations include:

  • Investigating how LLMs internally encode query–document relevance and whether these representations transfer across languages (CIKM 2026).
  • Studying whether LLMs develop shared, language-independent representations of problem difficulty (ACL 2026).
  • Developing a framework for measuring LLMs perspective alignment effectiveness across downstream applications (NLPerspectives@LREC 2026).
  • Influencing political ideology in LLMs using synthetic personas (The Web Conference 2025).
